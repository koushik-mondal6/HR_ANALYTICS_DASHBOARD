# HR_ANALYTICS_DASHBOARD
HR Analytics Dashboard: Employee Attrition & Demographics Tracker
Description
This dashboard provides a comprehensive visualization of workforce demographics and employee turnover. It enables HR professionals to analyze attrition drivers across departments, salary brackets, and tenure, delivering actionable insights to inform and improve employee retention strategies.

Tech Stack

Data Visualization & Modeling: Power BI (identified via the project's internal DataModel and Report/definition/report.json structure)   


Data Storage: Excel

Data Source
The project relies on the "HR.xlsx" dataset, which contains records for 1,473 employees, covering demographic attributes, job roles, salary groupings, and company tenure.   


Key Features & Highlights

Core KPIs: Tracks top-level metrics including total employee count (1.473K), total attrition (237), overall attrition rate (16.1%), average age (37), and average salary (6.5K).   


Departmental Filtering: Includes interactive slicers to isolate data across specific divisions: Human Resources, Research & Development, and Sales.   


Demographic Insights: Highlights attrition distribution by gender (150 Male vs. 87 Female) and identifies peak turnover occurring within the 26-35 age bracket.   


Compensation & Education Trends: Breaks down turnover by educational background (Life Sciences leads at 38%) and salary brackets, revealing the highest attrition volume (163 employees) in the 'Upto 5k' tier.   


Role & Tenure Tracking: Pinpoints high-turnover job roles, with Laboratory Technicians (62) and Sales Executives (57) leading the attrition count, while plotting turnover risk against total years spent at the company.
